## What the added diagnostics establish

- Early threshold fallback is material in the original sparse evaluation, but the expanded weekly evaluation has no fallback. Requiring three reference years retains only about 42% of original scored rows and all expanded weekly rows. This is a coverage finding, not proof of a better alert policy.
- The exploratory first-round climate conclusion is stable across the tested temporal block lengths. Those resamples reuse development predictions and do not create independent validation.
- Fresh original h8 replay confirms the tuning correction with exact execution settings. XGBoost refitting can change when thread count changes; saved six-thread predictions reproduce at six threads.
- Explicit annual SARIMA is now attempted on a predetermined subset; direct seasonal ARX is compared nationally. Neither is a comprehensive climate-exogenous seasonal SARIMAX study.
- Warning labels remain constructed Q75 events. Actual reporting arrivals, revision vintages, operational workload acceptance and health-user benefit are unresolved. Cross-period comparisons mixing full-climate expanding fits and frozen surveillance-only fits do not isolate temporal degradation of one fixed specification.
