The four approved models

  • Baseline/Naive — a simple reference forecast. Its role is a benchmark: other models must beat it. Strength: transparent and robust. Limitation: ignores climate and cross-district signal.
  • SARIMAX — a district-specific seasonal time-series model with climate regressors. Strength: captures per-district seasonality. Limitation: district-specific fitting is sensitive to short or gappy district histories.
  • Random Forest — a national-pooled tree ensemble. Role: the model selected for both count prediction and operational surge warning at all three horizons. Strength: captures non-linear interactions. Limitation: pooled importance may not describe every district.
  • XGBoost — a national-pooled gradient-boosted ensemble, evaluated as a count candidate but not selected in this dataset. Strength: regularised non-linear modelling. Limitation: importance is model-specific and not causal.

Roles above reflect published evidence. The exact selected model per horizon is shown live in the Model Selection tab, not hardcoded here; no metric is invented.
